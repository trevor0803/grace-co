Why Grace & Co.

Protection became personal before it became a profession.

As a mom, Teghan knows how important it is to protect the people we love and plan for the unexpected. That responsibility is what drew her to insurance and ultimately to Grace & Co. Insurance Consulting.

She primarily helps with life insurance, including final expense, and also offers health insurance options. Her goal is to make the process simple, honest and personal — no pressure, just real guidance.

For Teghan, insurance is more than a policy. It is a conversation about the people you care about, the responsibilities you carry and the future you want to protect.
