Frequently Asked Questions

Insurance, without the confusion.

Clear answers to some of the questions families ask most often.

How do I know what kind of coverage I need?

It depends on what you want to protect, your budget, your family situation and your goals. Teghan can help you sort through those priorities.

Is final expense insurance only for funeral costs?

It is commonly used for funeral and burial expenses, but policy proceeds may also help with other final bills or financial needs, subject to policy terms.

Will I be pressured to buy something?

No. Grace & Co. is built around explaining options clearly and helping you make an informed decision at your own pace.

Is coverage guaranteed?

No. Availability, eligibility and rates vary by carrier, state, age, health and policy terms, and coverage may be subject to underwriting and approval.

Can I ask questions before applying?

Absolutely. The goal is to make sure you understand the options before deciding what makes sense for you.

How do I get started?

Call, text or send Teghan a message. The first step is simply a conversation about what you are looking to protect.
